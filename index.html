Unrealized P&L
P&L = (Current − Avg Buy) × SharesMoney you'd make/lose selling everything today.
Return % & CAGR
Return % = (Current − Buy) / Buy × 100CAGR = (Current / Buy)^(1/years) − 1
PEG Ratio
PEG = P/E / EPS Growth RatePEG < 1 = growth is cheap. PEG > 2 = priced in.
Risk / Reward
R/R = (Target − Current) / (Current − Stop)

DCA Strategy Guide
What is Dollar-Cost Averaging?
DCA is an investment strategy where you invest a fixed amount at regular intervals (e.g., monthly), regardless of the asset's price. This reduces the impact of volatility by automatically buying more shares when prices are low and fewer when prices are high.
DCA vs Lump Sum
Studies show lump sum investing outperforms DCA ~66% of the time in bull markets, because markets tend to rise over time. However, DCA is superior for: psychological comfort, volatile markets, and investors without a large initial capital.
When to use DCA
✅ Regular income investors (monthly salary) · ✅ High-volatility assets · ✅ Uncertain market timing · ✅ Emotional risk management · ✅ Starting investors
